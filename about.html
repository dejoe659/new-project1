About DCF UNN

Dunamis Campus Fellowship is a student fellowship at the University of Nigeria, Nsukka, gathered around the Word, the Spirit and the work of reaching our campus.

Our vision

To win, to build, and to commission the campus community and Youth Corps Members as we prepare for the coming of our great God and Saviour, Jesus Christ.

Our mission

  • To lead students, staff and other members of the campus community to a personal encounter with our Saviour Jesus Christ through repentance.
  • To lead the campus community into deeper knowledge and personal experiences with Jesus, and to live holy, healthy, happy, balanced and fruitful Christian lives.
  • To get students and staff involved in practically fulfilling the Great Commission (Matthew 28:19-20, Mark 16:15-16).
  • To acquaint students and staff with basic Bible principles of success, to make a habit of succeeding in academics, career and endeavours.
  • To train students and staff to become God-approved Christian leaders (2 Timothy 2:15).
  • For massive generational impact by the youths.
  • Setting the campuses on fire for God.
  • Inflaming the youth to inflame the campus with revival.
  • Global revival via the Campus Fellowship.
  • Raising future ministry vessels (Genesis 14:14).
  • To liberate men from all the oppressions of the Devil.

Discovery of purpose. Activating potentials. Impacting the world.
